# AI Quick Reference

## For staff and volunteers at [Library Name]

*Print this. Post it at the service desk and in the staff workroom. Remember: beginning a review grants no permission, and approval of a product for one use does not authorize every feature that product offers.*

### You MAY undertake these low-risk internal uses within their authorized scope and recorded conditions

- Drafting and editing newsletters, social media posts, and routine announcements
- Brainstorming program ideas, instructional outlines, and booklist themes
- Researching general background context for programs and displays
- Proofreading and checking the grammar of nonconfidential written communications
- Creating administrative templates with aggregate or de-identified data
- Using specifically authorized low-risk embedded features in approved software within their recorded scope and conditions

### You MUST get an authorized decision first before

- Using any patron data or patron queries in a closed AI system
- Activating optional, newly added, or updated AI features in the ILS, discovery catalog, website, or subscription databases (even if the underlying platform is already approved)
- Moving from an authorized internal staff test or exercise to a live patron pilot or public activation
- Materially relying on AI outputs to shape patron access, search ranking, resource allocation, or public content
- Using AI to select, weed, classify, or rank materials for the collection
- Buying, subscribing to, or piloting a new AI tool for library workflows

### You must NEVER

- Enter confidential patron records, borrowing or search history, or identifiable reference questions into any unapproved or open AI tool
- Enter a patron's personal information, especially a minor's, into an open AI system
- Use AI to screen, rank, or evaluate job or volunteer candidates
- Use AI to profile, score, or predict the behavior of identifiable patrons
- Use AI to generate official library policy positions or collection decisions without authorized human review
- Use AI to create misleading, deceptive, or fabricated content

### How to report a concern or trigger reconsideration

- **Notice a vendor change, interpretation error, or patron complaint?** Record enough context to investigate without collecting unnecessary patron reading histories, and report it to the designated **Service Owner** or **Registry Maintainer**.
- **Pause on scope changes:** If a vendor update adds unapproved behavior (such as personalized ranking or new data processing) that cannot be isolated, pause the affected feature and escalate to technology staff and the director.
- **Preserve alternatives:** Always offer patrons a clear non-AI service route (standard catalog search or staff reference assistance).

### Always remember

- **You remain accountable** for the service and content you provide, even when an AI feature assisted.
- **AI output is a starting point, not an authority.** Verify accuracy and check what perspectives or holdings may be missed.
- **The key questions are always:** What data am I putting into this use, whose access does it shape, and what is its current authorized disposition in the registry?
