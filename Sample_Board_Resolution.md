# Sample Board Resolution

## Ready-to-read motion language for adopting the AI Use Policy

*For the board secretary or director. Adapt the bracketed terms to your library's governance structure. Some libraries adopt policy by board resolution at a public meeting; municipal or county libraries may route adoption or specific contracting and risk-acceptance powers through a parent agency or consortium. Identify where actual decision authority resides under your bylaws.*

### Motion

> **Moved**, that the Board of Trustees of [Library Name] adopt the Artificial Intelligence Use Policy, Version 0.3.0, dated [Date], as presented; and
>
> that the [director / designated stewardship lead or working group] be directed to carry out a staged first implementation cycle within agreed staff workload allocations to:
>
> 1. **Establish scope and authority** by assigning local stewardship functions (service owner, reviewer, approver, registry maintainer, records custodian, and authorized risk acceptor where applicable) and establishing an **AI Tool & Use Registry** that records authorized dispositions separately from review activity;
> 2. **Develop evidence and participation** by evaluating consequential AI-enabled uses and vendor-added AI features across Accuracy, Autonomy, Recourse, and Enforceability—incorporating proportionate community and staff participation in defining service problems, alternatives, and evaluation criteria—before any public activation;
> 3. **Decide and communicate** by translating authorized dispositions and conditions into actionable staff guidance, accessible public explanations, and clear correction or reconsideration routes; and
> 4. **Examine feasibility** at the close of the first implementation cycle—reviewing actual staff effort, procedural clarity, record reconstructability, and responses to community and staff contributions—and report at least annually to the Board on active registry dispositions, any time-limited exceptions, and stewardship feasibility; and
>
> that this Policy be reviewed and updated at least once per year, or sooner as technological, vendor, or legal developments warrant.

### Supporting note for the agenda packet (optional)

Artificial intelligence features are already present in the library's everyday software, subscription databases, and discovery systems. This Policy gives [Library Name] a practical, values-grounded stewardship structure for governing AI-enabled uses: protecting patron privacy and intellectual freedom, separating evaluation activity from permission to operate, involving community knowledge and staff experience in consequential choices, and maintaining a reconstructable record of what is permitted, under which conditions, and who can revise the decision.

Rather than imposing an arbitrary calendar deadline, the resolution directs a four-stage implementation cycle paced to local staff capacity. The minimum it requires is one maintained registry and one rule: no AI-enabled use reaches patrons, patron data, or consequential decisions without an explicit authorized disposition.
