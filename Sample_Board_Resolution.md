# Sample Board Resolution

## Ready-to-read motion language for adopting the AI Use Policy

*For the board secretary or director. Adapt the bracketed terms to your library's governance structure. Some libraries adopt policy by board resolution at a public meeting; municipal libraries may route adoption through the city or county. Use the path your bylaws require.*

---

### Motion

> **Moved**, that the Board of Trustees of [Library Name] adopt the Artificial Intelligence Use Policy, Version 1.0, dated [Date], as presented; and
>
> that the [director / designated committee] be directed to:
>
> 1. establish and maintain an AI tool registry recording the AI-enabled tools and features in use across the library;
> 2. review high-risk AI uses and new vendor-added AI features in accordance with Section VII of the Policy before they are activated;
> 3. provide staff training on the Policy within 60 days of adoption; and
> 4. report to the Board at least annually on the state of AI use at [Library Name]; and
>
> that this Policy be reviewed and updated at least once per year, or sooner as developments warrant.

---

### Supporting note for the agenda packet (optional)

AI tools are already present in the library's everyday software, its subscription databases, and its public services. This Policy gives the library a clear, values-grounded structure for deciding what to do with them: protecting patron privacy and intellectual freedom, guiding staff use, and keeping a simple record of which tools are in use and on what terms. It is designed to scale to the library's size. The minimum it requires is one maintained registry and one rule: no AI tool reaches patrons or patron data without an entry.

The Policy is adapted from a freely licensed (CC BY 4.0) template and is consistent with the American Library Association's Guidance on the Use of Artificial Intelligence in Libraries (2026). It is an independent resource and not an official publication of any library association.
