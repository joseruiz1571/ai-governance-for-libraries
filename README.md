# AI Governance for Public Libraries

A practical, values-grounded AI governance kit for public libraries, free to use and adapt under CC BY 4.0.

---

## What This Is

Public libraries hold the trust of the people they serve: what patrons read, search, ask, and borrow is confidential by professional ethics and, in most states, by law. As AI tools become embedded in everyday software, in the catalog, and in subscription databases, libraries need clear guidance on what to do with them.

This kit gives a library's board and director what they need to adopt an AI use policy, evaluate new tools, train staff, and keep a simple record of what is in use. Nothing here requires technical knowledge, and nothing costs money.

It is the companion to the article "From AI Literacy to AI Stewardship: A Public Library Governance Model" (Library Leadership & Management).

---

## What Is In the Kit

**AI_Use_Policy_Template.md**
The full policy template, ready for the board to adapt and adopt. Eleven sections covering definitions, professional grounding, approved and prohibited uses, intellectual freedom, patron privacy, data stewardship, oversight, training, and public literacy. Replace the bracketed library name, adjust what does not fit, and it is ready for a vote.

**AI_Tool_Registry_Template.md**
The keystone. A working template for the one record every library should keep: the AI tools and features in use, who owns each, what data it touches, and on what terms. Built on the discipline libraries already know, authority control, so it does not decay into a static spreadsheet.

**AI_Tool_Evaluation_Checklist.md**
A short checklist for evaluating any new AI tool, or any vendor-added AI feature, before approval. Its output becomes a registry entry. Carries the four-question impact review.

**AI_Quick_Reference_OnePager.md**
A single printable page: what staff may use AI for freely, what requires approval, and what is never permitted. Post it at the desk.

**Sample_Board_Resolution.md**
Ready-to-read motion language for adopting the policy at a board meeting.

**AI_Literacy_Deck_Outline.md**
A 13-slide literacy outline for board meetings, staff training, or public programming. Plain-language explanations of what AI is, where it already lives in the library, and where the real risks are.

## How to Use It

1. Hand the policy template to two or three people and adapt it to your library's context and governance structure.
2. Inventory the AI tools and features your staff and your vendors are already using. You will be surprised. Start the registry.
3. Adopt the policy at a board meeting using the included resolution.
4. Train staff and walk volunteers through the one-pager.
5. Assign someone to maintain the registry and review new tools going forward.

That is a realistic 60-to-90-day arc, and the kit contains what those five steps need.

The minimum that makes this real is one maintained registry and one rule: no AI tool reaches patrons or patron data without an entry.
