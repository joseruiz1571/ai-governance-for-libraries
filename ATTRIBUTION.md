# Attribution and branding decision

*Internal note. Not part of the published kit.*

## Decision history (2026-06-15 through 2026-11-10)

The kit ships **neutral and openly licensed (CC BY 4.0)**, not Field 83-branded, at least to start.

* **Original creator attribution (`v0.1.0` – `v0.2.0`):** José Ruiz-Vazquez. The materials were initially ported from a congregations kit created by José; provenance for that kit is documented in the companion repo. Version `0.2.0` (`doi: 10.5281/zenodo.22330686`) remains sole-authored by José Ruiz-Vazquez as cited in the companion article's historical reference.

* **Co-credit and R1 alignment (`v0.3.0`, 2026-11-10):** Aligned with the revised *Public Library Quarterly* companion article (*"AI Stewardship in Public Libraries: A Proposed Governance Model"* by Witt Salley and José Ruiz-Vazquez). Version `0.3.0` updates the footer language and `CITATION.cff` to include co-author Witt Salley while preserving José Ruiz-Vazquez as original creator, and incorporates the revised model's separation of authorized disposition and review state, proportionate community and staff participation fields, four-stage completion-based feasibility cycle, and administrative measures.

* **Field 83, deferred:** The Field 83 imprint is held in reserve. CC BY 4.0 preserves optionality indefinitely.

## Why neutral now

CC BY lets us be generous without giving up control. First billing, co-credit, and a no-brand presentation cost nothing, because the license keeps every later move open. Generosity now, optionality forever.

## Open thread: a neutral home on the web

José floated standing up a small neutral website to point people to (2026-06-15). Natural fit: this repo is already markdown under CC BY, so a static site (GitHub Pages or similar) pointing to the kit is low effort and on-brand.
